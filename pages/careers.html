CAREER FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can students apply for internships? +

Yes. Students and early-career candidates can apply for relevant internship or project-based opportunities. Your academic background does not have to be directly related to the role if you can demonstrate relevant skills or projects.

Do I need professional experience? +

Not necessarily. For internships and early-career opportunities, academic projects, personal projects, GitHub repositories, portfolios and demonstrated skills can be useful.

Can I apply for more than one role? +

Yes, but apply thoughtfully. Select roles that genuinely match your skills and interests rather than applying to every available position.

Can I send my profile if there is no suitable opening? +

Yes. If you have a useful skill or believe you can contribute to RentBook India, you can submit your profile through the application section.

What should I include in my application? +

Include your resume, relevant skills, projects, portfolio or GitHub links and a short explanation of why you are interested in the role.

Is remote work available? +

Work arrangements will depend on the specific role, project requirements and stage of the organization. The final arrangement should be clearly communicated during the hiring process.
