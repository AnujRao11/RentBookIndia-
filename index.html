QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

RentBook India is a technology-enabled marketplace designed to connect students with books available for rental through participating local bookstores.

Search for a book, select an available rental duration, review the price and applicable deposit, place the order, receive the book, use it and return it according to the rental terms.

Rental duration can vary by book and bookstore. Planned options include 7, 15, 30, 60 and 90 days, subject to actual inventory and rental policies.

Some books may require a refundable security deposit. The exact amount and refund conditions should be displayed before checkout.

Eligible deposits can be refunded after the book is returned and inspected, according to the applicable rental and damage policy.

Late returns may be subject to a late fee according to the rental terms associated with the book.

Renewal may be available when the book is not reserved by another customer and the bookstore supports renewal.
